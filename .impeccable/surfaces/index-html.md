# Surface brief: Night Ledger

Mode: **Read** (a visitor reads one person's record of two dishes; the diary is the front door).

## Scope and audience

Named target `index.html`. Covers Diary (default), Map, and Entry. A stranger arrives on a phone from a shared link; the owner edits `data/entries.js` by hand. Product truth, the two dish names, the `#/e/` links, Leaflet, GitHub Pages, and no new dependencies are unchanged.

## Job and proof

Answer "where did this person eat, and what was it like" within seconds. Proof: the newest visit is a tipped-in photograph on a mat, carrying an ink date stamp and a dish stamp; a numbered print strip leads to older visits; a pin or row opens the same record; the detail page shows the large photo and the typed note.

## States and ranges

1 to 100+ entries; one or multiple photos; optional rating and price; empty diary; filtered-to-empty; missing image; map tile failure; no coordinates. Desktop popup and phone sheet carry the same content. Reduced motion and keyboard paths are binding.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private ledger of real visits, shown as tipped-in prints. It refuses the uniform card grid and the accent-coloured dashboard; the record itself is the interface.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black cloth ground #111316, binding #0b0c0e, warm off-white photo mat #d8d2c4, ink #ded8cc, one brass accent #9a8768. Oxblood #7a4a46 and sage #6f7a5c belong only to the two dish stamps. Spectral for display and reading, Courier Prime for stamps, dates, folios and labels. Matted prints, double-rule date stamps, a 66px binding rail, a numbered print strip. Dish identity tints a whole entry region, never a scattered chip.

STORY: The visitor lands on a dark page with one large photograph on a paper mat, reads the stamped date and dish, reads the owner's line beside it, follows the print strip or a stamp to other visits, and leaves knowing this is one named person's record, not a reviews site.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 66px binding rail at the left with a brass bowl mark and a vertical folio; a masthead with the wordmark and Diary/Map page tabs; the newest visit as a large matted photograph on the left with a plate label; on the right a double-ruled ink date stamp, a dish stamp, the shop name in large Spectral, a typed note, and one underlined "Read entry" action; along the foot a horizontal strip of numbered print thumbnails with the current one brass-ruled. Dish filters sit as three plain stamped labels in the masthead. The primary action is the photograph.

FORM: Ledger and tipped-in print, position 1 of the grounded list. seed:b28160b94763f696. Raised from the hand it beat: the 1:1 detail call-out, one dish control that re-marks every matching entry, one hand-ink annotation, a persistent "you were here" tape flag, colour as the sheet rather than the mark, and monumental counts for dish labels.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
