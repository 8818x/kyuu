# Surface brief: Paper Editorial

Mode: **Read** (a visitor reads one person's record of two dishes; the diary is the front door).

## Scope and audience

Named target `index.html`. Covers Diary (default), Map, and Entry. A stranger arrives on a phone from a shared link; the owner edits `data/entries.js` by hand. Product truth, the two dish names, the `#/e/` links, Leaflet, GitHub Pages, and no new dependencies are unchanged. This is a replacement visual world, not a refresh of the discarded dark ledger.

## Job and proof

Answer "where did this person eat, and what was it like" within seconds. Proof: the newest visit is a large photograph on a white paper mount, a bold condensed-free serif name, a red rule, the date, and the owner's note. The print strip and the map list lead to other visits.

## States and ranges

1 to 100+ entries; one or multiple photos; optional rating and price; empty diary; filtered-to-empty; missing image; map tile failure; no coordinates. Desktop popup and phone sheet carry the same content. Reduced motion and keyboard paths are binding.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A white broadsheet with black ink and one red rule. It refuses the dark gallery, the card grid, and the coloured dashboard; the page is set like a serious newspaper and the photograph is the only luxuriance.

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint #f7f5ef ground, #ece8de surface, white photo mount, ink #161514, secondary #6a655e, one vermilion #d23b2a signal, hairline #d5cfc4 rules. Bodoni Moda for display, Archivo for running text, DM Mono for dates, folios and labels. Square corners, no cards, no shadows, one red rule under the active tab. The red appears on controls and marks only, never as a background fill.

STORY: The visitor lands on a light page where a single large food photograph dominates the first fold. They read the shop name and the typed note, follow the ruled print strip or a filter to another visit, and open the map to find the place. They leave knowing this is one person's considered record, printed rather than posted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim left rail with a red bowl mark and a vertical folio; a masthead with the wordmark and Diary/Map tabs; the newest visit as a large white-mounted photograph on the left with a red plate label; on the right a red hairline above the shop name in large Bodoni, a DM Mono date and dish label, the note, and one underlined red action; along the foot a ruled strip of numbered prints with the current one underlined in red. The primary action is the photograph.

FORM: Paper Editorial, chosen from a fifteen-direction discovery set. seed: none (unavailable). The world keeps only the disciplines that serve print: the hairline rule, the tabular label, the single red signal, and the photograph as the only image on the page.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
