## Overall Impression

Clean, warm and correct, and the read loop works end to end. But the default view undersells it: a stock map with one anonymous blue pin, while the site's whole idea (two dishes) lives one tab away. Two real defects undercut it: the flagship jump can land on an empty filter, and every tab or filter click drops keyboard focus.

## What's Working

1. The read loop exists and works: pin, popup, Open in diary, highlighted card, with reduced-motion respected.
2. Contrast and control basics are solid: every sampled pair passes AA, all pills are 44px tall, focus-visible ring renders on tabs, filters, zoom and markers, and markers are keyboard-reachable with Enter opening the popup.
3. Card composition and the illustrated bowl: image, tag, name, date, note, collapsing to one column under 600px.
