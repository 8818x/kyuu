# Two new identities for Food Diary

Preview gallery: [.impeccable/previews/compare.html](.impeccable/previews/compare.html)

Preview only. No source file was changed. The screenshots are the live Diary and Map pages with a temporary stylesheet injected in the browser, at 1440×900 and 390×844. Everything shown here is a proposal to choose from, not the current site.

Both directions are dark, photograph-first, and use two muted dish marks only. Neither reuses brass, oxblood, sage, or the old orange. The example content is the one real sample entry; no shop, photo, price, or claim was invented.

## A · Nocturne Signal

**Mood and colour.** A quiet after-dark gallery. The ground is ink-violet `#0e0e16`, raised surfaces `#171725`, text cool bone `#e6e4ef`, secondary `#a5a4bb`. One periwinkle signal `#8f93c8` carries every action. The two dishes use cool steel `#6f7fae` and cool teal `#6f9aa8`. Pale lilac-grey `#cfcbdd` is the only light field, used as a photo mount. Nothing is warm, earthy, or café-like.

**Visual style.** Geometric Archivo headings, DM Mono labels, tight hairline rules, a framed illuminated photo panel, and generous dark space. The rail and folio stay, but they read as an exhibition index, not a ledger. Controls are underlined text, never filled blocks.

**Contrast (WCAG AA):** ink/ground 15.29:1 · muted/ground 7.89:1 · accent/ground 6.56:1 · pale mount ink 8.83:1 · ink/surface 14.09:1 · muted/surface 7.28:1. All pass.

**Raised by the hand it beat:** one calm caption panel over the image field (from the festival hoarding) · a strict module that photographs snap into (from the Dumbar identity) · one persistent "you were here" mark (from the cutting bench) · type as structure, not decoration (from the alphabet storm).

**Honest risk:** with only one sample photograph, the cool restraint may read as empty rather than calm. It needs real food photographs to reach its full effect.

## B · Cyanotype Contact Archive

**Mood and colour.** A cool photographic archive. Deep prussian ground `#071420`, raised `#102234`, ice text `#dce9f2`, secondary `#9db5c7`. One cyan-blue signal `#7fb6cf`; dishes use muted `#6f9fb8` and `#8fa5a0`. Ice-pale paper `#d5e3ec` mounts every photograph. The palette comes from cyanotype printing, not from food or café interiors.

**Visual style.** Condensed uppercase Barlow Condensed labels, Source Sans 3 reading text, cyan hairlines, numbered contact frames, and a strict grid. The Diary reads like a contact sheet; the Map reads like an indexed plate cabinet. Very different from A at a glance: A is spacious and gallery-like, B is dense and systematic.

**Contrast (WCAG AA):** ink/ground 15.03:1 · muted/ground 8.73:1 · accent/ground 8.40:1 · pale mount ink 10.38:1 · ink/surface 13.06:1 · muted/surface 7.59:1. All pass.

**Honest risk:** this is the more familiar direction — the one most runs in this category land on. Effective and safe, but less of a break from the past.

## Which is stronger

**A · Nocturne Signal** is the recommendation. It makes a clean break from every world this project has tried; B is the safe, highly legible alternative. The concept roll assigned A, and its cool palette satisfies the brief's refusal of warm and earthy tones.

## What was considered and set aside

My seven grounded candidates, in resonance order: **Cyanotype Contact Archive**, **Blue Hour Gallery**, **Nocturne Signal**, **Slate Proof**, **Midnight Aquarium**, **Violet Index**, **Graphite Darkroom**. The roll assigned candidate 3, so A is built from Nocturne Signal; Cyanotype is shown as the single pick card.

The six dealt challengers were all declined after fusing them to this product: the drawn zine (a mascot would invent the owner's voice), the film cutting bench (orange flag and an editing workflow this site does not have), the alphabet storm (white ground breaks the dark requirement), the transforming cape (black and gold reads warm), the Dumbar identity (saturated CMYK blocks break the restrained-accent rule), and the festival hoarding (loud warm print with no photography). Each donated one discipline, listed on A above. Their clothes were not borrowed.
